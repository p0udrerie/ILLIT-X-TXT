Declassified records of the Fatui Vanguard

Thirst for Combat

Childe is unique among the Harbingers: unlike his conspiratorial peers, he is motivated purely by the thrill of fighting stronger opponents. Having fallen into the Abyss at age 14, he learned lethal martial arts under Skirk, sharpening his combat instincts to perfection.

Family Devotion

Behind the feared harbinger lies a gentle elder brother named Ajax. He adores his younger siblings—Teucer, Tonia, and Anthon—writing them frequent letters from his travels and protecting their innocent belief that he is merely a "toy seller in Liyue".

Vision & Delusion

Mastering the bow specifically because it is the weapon he is worst at, Childe converts Hydro elemental energy into slicing daggers and polearms. When pushed to his limits, he activates his Electro Delusion and the devastating Foul Legacy armor.
