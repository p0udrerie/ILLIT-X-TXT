Group Profiles

Get to know the magical concept and origin of both groups

BELIFT LAB Debut: March 25, 2024

ILLIT

Formed through the survival show 'R U Next?', ILLIT is a 5-member girl group embodying "I WILL BE IT"—expressing potential to become anything you imagine. Known for whimsical, dreamcore visual aesthetics and viral hyper-pop hits like "Magnetic".

Fandom Name: GLLIT (글릿)
Key Vibe: Dreamy, Cute, Nostalgic, Whimsical
BIGHIT MUSIC Debut: March 4, 2019

TXT

TOMORROW X TOGETHER is a 5-member powerhouse group where "you and I come together under one dream to build a better tomorrow." Famed for unique storytelling, intricate concepts, high energy performances, and genre-defying pop-rock tracks.

Fandom Name: MOA (Moments Of Alwaysness)
Key Vibe: Futuristic, Pop-Rock, Storytelling, Youth
